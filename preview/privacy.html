Privacy policy

Watch Faces · Last updated September 29, 2026

Watch Faces is a companion app for browsing validated watch faces and sending a selected face to a paired Wear OS watch. This policy describes information handled by the app.

Information handled by the app

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated account service. It stores the Automatic updates preference on your phone and temporarily caches watch-face packages in app storage.

To show available designs, the app requests the public release catalog and selected watch-face files from GitHub. GitHub receives the network request and may process technical connection information such as your IP address under its privacy statement. The app does not send your name, email address, location, or contacts to GitHub.

When you install or update a face, the app sends the selected watch-face package and installation metadata to your paired watch using Google Play services Wearable Data Layer. Depending on device connectivity, that transfer may be relayed through Google services and is end-to-end encrypted. Google Play services may process information as described in Google's privacy policy.

Storage and retention

The app does not operate a server that stores your personal information. Downloaded face packages are kept in app cache and may be removed by Android. The Automatic updates setting remains in app-private storage until you change it or uninstall the app.

Your choices

You can turn off Automatic updates in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its private settings and cached files. Network access is used to retrieve the public catalog and face packages.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the maintainer through the project issue tracker. Please do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
